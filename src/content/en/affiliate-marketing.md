## What is affiliate marketing?

Affiliate marketing means promoting someone else's product or service through a unique tracking link, and earning a commission when someone buys through that link. You don't create the product, hold inventory, or handle post-sale support — the merchant does that.

## How it works

1. You join an affiliate program (an e-commerce store, a SaaS tool, a hosting company, etc.).
2. You get a tracking link with your own referral code.
3. You share that link through a blog, videos, social media, or email.
4. If someone clicks and buys within the program's "cookie window" (often 7–90 days), you earn a commission.

## Why most beginners quit

- **Picking the wrong niche** — chasing a "hot" topic they don't actually understand or have an audience for.
- **Unrealistic expectations** — expecting income in month one, while SEO and traffic take time to build.
- **Spamming links** — dropping links everywhere with no real value, which platforms throttle and readers ignore.
- **No measurement** — never tracking click-through or conversion rate to improve the content.

## How to start the right way

Pick a topic you genuinely understand or are actively learning, create content that solves a specific problem (comparisons, how-tos, honest reviews), then place your affiliate link naturally inside it. Staying consistent for 3–6 months matters far more than chasing a "growth hack."

> Note: affiliate income is not guaranteed and depends heavily on traffic, trust, and the program you join. This is shared experience, not an income promise.
