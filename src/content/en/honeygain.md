## What is Honeygain?

Honeygain is an app that lets you **share your device's unused internet bandwidth** (computer, phone) in exchange for "credits," which you can later cash out. Honeygain resells that network capacity to companies that need it for public web data collection, market research, and similar uses. You don't do anything beyond installing the app and letting it run in the background.

This is a **very small passive-income stream**, best used as a side add-on for devices that are already sitting idle — **not a primary income source**.

## How it works & the credit system

According to Honeygain's own payout page:

- **10,000 credits = $10 USD**, so **1,000 credits ≈ $1 USD**.
- Credits accrue as your device securely shares bandwidth in the background (it's not supposed to slow down your own connection).
- The accrual rate depends on **demand in your region at that time**, how long your device stays online, and how many devices you connect — Honeygain **does not publish a fixed credits-per-GB rate**.

## A multi-device earning plan

The key mechanic: Honeygain rewards **traffic volume plus IP/network diversity**, so running several devices on **the same home Wi-Fi** is far less effective than spreading them across **different networks**. A sensible way to scale up gradually:

| Stage | Device | Network | Note |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Your main computer/laptop | Home Wi-Fi | Install and try it, leave it running if the machine stays on |
| 2 | An old spare phone | 4G/5G (its own SIM) | Keep it charging, screen locked, running in the background |
| 3 | A laptop/phone at work or a relative's place (with permission) | A network different from home | Each distinct network is a separate credit source |
| 4 | A secondary device (Raspberry Pi, old tablet...) | Mobile data/café or dorm Wi-Fi | Prefer low-power devices that can stay on continuously |

Important: Honeygain supports Windows, macOS, Linux (including via Docker) and Android. **There is no official iOS app** as of this writing. Before running it on a work or school network, check that network's usage policy — some organizations prohibit this kind of bandwidth-sharing software.

## Minimum payout & how to withdraw

Honeygain currently offers two payout paths:

| Method | Minimum | Payout via | Processing time |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Standard payout (PayPal)** | 20,000 credits (= $20) | PayPal | Per Honeygain's FAQ: roughly 2–3 business days |
| **JumpTask Mode** | 500 credits (= $0.50) | Gift cards, crypto, or PayPal via JumpTask | Advertised as "fast, low-fee" (no exact figure given) |

**Steps to withdraw via PayPal (the most common path):**

1. Open the Honeygain app/extension → go to **Payout**.
2. Link your PayPal account (your PayPal email).
3. Once your balance reaches the 20,000-credit minimum, tap **Request Payout**.
4. Wait for Honeygain to process it (typically a few business days) — the money lands in your PayPal wallet.
5. Per Honeygain's FAQ, PayPal itself may charge a small transaction fee; Honeygain does not add any extra fee.

**If you want to cash out below $20:** link a JumpTask account inside the app and switch to JumpTask Mode to withdraw from as little as $0.50, via gift card, crypto, or PayPal.

## Risks & honest caveats

- **Earnings are very small** relative to the effort — fine as a side add-on for idle devices, not something to expect as a real income replacement.
- **Check your network's usage policy** (ISP, employer, school) before running it — some networks or organizations don't allow this kind of bandwidth-sharing software.
- **Security**: only install from the official honeygain.com site; don't grant device permissions to any unofficial app claiming to be "Honeygain."
- Payout policy, minimums, and the referral program **can change** — always check the current terms in the app or on the official site before making a decision.
