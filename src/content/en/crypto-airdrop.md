## What is an airdrop?

An airdrop is when a blockchain project distributes free tokens to users who already interacted with its product (using the app, providing liquidity, testing a testnet, etc.). It's a way for projects to attract early users.

## Risks to watch for

- **Fake wallets/links** — scammers build fake "claim your airdrop" pages to steal your seed phrase or wallet access. **Never** type your seed phrase into any website.
- **Fake airdrops asking for upfront payment** — a real airdrop never asks you to send money or tokens to "unlock your reward."
- **Value volatility** — the token you receive can lose value quickly after listing; don't treat it as guaranteed income.

## How to participate safely

Use a separate "hot" wallet dedicated to airdrop activity, holding no significant assets. Only interact through official links from the project's verified site/Twitter/Discord, and always double-check the contract address yourself before signing any transaction.

> This is a high-risk, fast-moving space. This content is shared experience, not investment advice.
