## The dropshipping model

You sell through your own online store but never hold inventory. When an order comes in, you purchase it from a supplier (often overseas) who ships directly to the customer. Your profit is the margin between your sale price and the supplier's price.

## Costs people often overlook

- **Ad spend** — usually the largest cost, often 20–40% of revenue when running paid ads.
- **Return/cancellation rate** — slow overseas shipping leads to higher return rates than traditional retail.
- **Platform & payment fees** — Shopify and payment gateways both charge transaction fees.

## Why so many stores close early

Most beginners underestimate ad-testing costs and run out of budget before finding product-market fit. Dropshipping needs real capital for ad testing — it isn't the "zero capital" model it's often advertised as.

## How to start sensibly

If you want to try it, set a clear minimum budget just for ad testing (separate from inventory cost), and treat the first 1–2 months as a learning phase, not a profit phase.
