## Main income sources

- **Platform ads** (YouTube Partner Program, TikTok Creator Rewards) — paid per qualifying view, with minimum follower/watch-hour thresholds.
- **Brand deals** — cash or product in exchange for promotion, usually once your channel has a steady audience.
- **In-video affiliate links** — linking relevant products in the description or a video shoutout.
- **Your own products/services** — using the channel to build a personal brand around what you sell.

## A realistic timeline

Most channels take 6–12 months of consistent posting before views stabilize. Platform ad revenue is usually tiny early on; the bigger income tends to come from brand deals and affiliate work once the channel has credibility in a specific niche.

## Practical advice

Pick a niche you can sustain long-term, invest in basic editing/audio quality before chasing "viral," and track metrics like retention and CTR to improve each video.
