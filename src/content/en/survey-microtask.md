## What are microtasks?

Small, repetitive jobs: answering surveys, labeling data, testing websites, rating search results. Platforms like Prolific, Clickworker, or market-research survey panels pay per small task completed.

## Realistic earnings

This is **not** a primary income source. Hourly pay is usually much lower than freelancing or skilled work — think of it as pocket-money for spare time, not a living.

## How to spot a trustworthy platform

- A legitimate platform **never** charges a fee to "unlock" tasks or to withdraw earnings.
- It has a clear payout policy, a reasonable minimum threshold, and independent user reviews you can actually find.
- Avoid sites asking for an upfront deposit, recruiting "downlines" to rank up, or promising unusually fixed returns — these are classic scam signals.

## Bottom line

Microtasks are fine for a bit of spare-time cash, but shouldn't be treated as a long-term primary income source.
