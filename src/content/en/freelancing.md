## Who is freelancing a good fit for?

If you already have a concrete skill — writing, design, translation, coding, video editing, data entry — freelancing is the fastest way to turn that skill into income without any upfront capital.

## Three steps to start

1. **Pick a specific service.** Don't list "I can do a lot of things" — choose one or two clear offers, like "product description writing for e-commerce."
2. **Build a portfolio.** With no clients yet, create 2–3 sample pieces to prove your skill.
3. **Pick the right platform.** Upwork and Fiverr for the international market; local freelancer communities for domestic clients.

## Tips for landing your first job

- Price below market for your first 2–3 jobs to earn reviews, then raise your rate gradually.
- Write a tailored proposal for each job — avoid copy-pasting the same pitch.
- Respond quickly and clearly; it's the trait clients value most in freelancers.

## Pitfalls to avoid

Be wary of "jobs" that ask you to pay a fee upfront, or payment arranged outside the platform (you lose dispute protection). Always agree on the scope of work in writing before starting.
